# Alpha Hunt Controller Protocol

## Objective

Keep Botalin's research loop moving until independently verified sleeves can be evaluated for a multi-strategy router. The goal is evidence-backed candidates, not a forced positive backtest.

## Non-idle rule

At least one bounded item must exist in each applicable queue:

1. **Data health**: a concrete missing field, freshness defect, or execution-data gap.
2. **Discovery**: a distinct payer mechanism or structural variant.
3. **Validation**: ideal-fill, OOS, null, robustness, or execution-replay test.
4. **Integration**: causal dataset, router, correlation, or regime-admission work.

If a queue is blocked by a running recorder or calendar checkpoint, the controller must immediately choose a non-overlapping item from another queue. A blocked recorder never pauses the programme.

## Alpha's required output per cycle

For each cycle Alpha must deliver a ranked queue of three executable research items. Every item names:

- payer and causal mechanism;
- market pocket, regime, universe, timeframe, entry and exit;
- exact non-overlap check against known families;
- minimum source data and decision-time fields;
- ideal-fill-first and then execution/OOS gates;
- failure routes: structural variant, data request, guard-only, quarantine, or reject.

Alpha cannot promote, start paper/live, modify runtime infrastructure, or call a result profitable.

## Pattern-first discovery rule

Discovery may begin from a recurring observable price structure rather than a pre-declared payer. The controller must actively mine large normalized moves, reversals, compressions, expansions, gaps, failed continuations, and session transitions across symbols and timeframes.

For a pattern-first item, the first requirements are a precise event definition, repeated historical occurrences, decision-time observables, entry, exit, and a falsifiable out-of-sample test. A payer or market-mechanism explanation is recorded afterwards as an explanatory hypothesis; it is never a prerequisite for asking whether a repeatable pattern exists. It becomes important only when deciding whether a surviving pattern is structurally credible enough to advance beyond replay.

## Orchestrator cadence

Every two hours, and after any task completion:

1. inspect the four queues and active data collectors;
2. accept or reject completed work against its stated gates;
3. dispatch the highest-value non-overlapping task;
4. record the next owner and checkpoint;
5. keep `NO_TRADE` and zero allocation unless an independent sleeve earns admission.

## Anti-stagnation

- A negative result closes only its precise rule set, never the whole mechanism space.
- Alpha must propose a causal structural variant before a family is retired, unless data truth says the source is inadequate.
- A running task without a concrete deliverable or checkpoint is interrupted and replaced.
- A completed agent that has no successor is assigned the next bounded queue item.

## Safety boundary

Research automation may create docs, scripts, read-only replay results, and task handoffs. It cannot create orders, paper runs, services, timers, approval files, coordinator changes, or capital changes. Every such action still needs explicit operator GO.
