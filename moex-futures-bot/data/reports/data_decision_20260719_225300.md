- MOEX ISS equivalent for this close: last intraday futures candle close for the

## Interpretation

Finam daily bars are broker-facing last-trade daily bars including late/evening
trading. MOEX ISS history `close` and interval-24 candle `close` are not the
same convention; they can differ by 4-9% on volatile days.

For strategy research that aims to match broker bars, use:

- Finam daily bars when available;
- or MOEX ISS intraday candles aggregated to last candle close.

For settlement/funding research, use:

- MOEX ISS `settleprice`;
- MOEX ISS `SWAPRATE`;
- MOEX ISS contract params such as `BUYSELLFEE`, `MINSTEP`, and `STEPPRICE`.

useful for settlement-style research, but not ideal for broker-close strategy
